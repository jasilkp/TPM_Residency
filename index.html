Frequently Asked Questions

Essential property information, location details, and direct contact guidance for our upcoming guests.

Is TPM Residency related to or the same as Thekkumpuram Residency?

No. TPM Residency is an independent luxury hospitality property located at TPM Tower, Pazhayangadi, Kondotty (NH 966) under the TPM Group. It has no affiliation, connection, or relation to Thekkumpuram Residency (located in Kumminiparamba). They are two distinct, independent properties.

Where is TPM Residency located?

TPM Residency is located at C15–C27, TPM Tower, KM-445, Pazhayangadi Town Hub, Kondotty, Malappuram, Kerala – 673638. Our property has direct highway access via NH 966 and is approximately 10 to 15 minutes drive from Calicut International Airport (CCJ).

What accommodations and amenities does TPM Residency feature?

TPM Residency offers contemporary air-conditioned rooms and suites, dedicated 24-hour guest assistance, an executive conference hall, private secure parking, power backup, and quiet lounge spaces crafted for both airport stopovers and business visits.

How can I contact TPM Residency for reservations or opening inquiries?

You can reach our team directly by phone at +91 79028 88990 or +91 73064 24954, or via email at tpmresidency@gmail.com. Directions can also be viewed on Google Maps.
